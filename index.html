WordBank Prep — free SAT & ACT vocabulary practice. Words are drawn from the vocabulary commonly found on major SAT/ACT prep lists (Barron's, Kaplan, Princeton Review, Direct Hits, and others) plus the "words in context" vocabulary tested on the current digital SAT and ACT. Tiers: Core = highest-frequency words to master first · Extended = words found on standard prep lists · Advanced = challenging words for top scorers. Words tagged SAT-only are classic hard SAT-list words less likely to matter on the ACT.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.
